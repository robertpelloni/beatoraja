What are Skins?

beatoraja uses skins to define the visual layout of every screen. You can customize the look and feel by selecting different skins for different parts of the game.

Skin Categories

  • 7KEYS / 5KEYS: Gameplay screen for standard modes.
  • 14KEYS / 10KEYS: Gameplay screen for double modes.
  • MUSIC SELECT: The song selection screen.
  • DECIDE: Transition animation before gameplay.
  • RESULT: Score summary screen after a song.
  • COURSE RESULT: Summary screen for course mode.

Customizing Skins

Within the Skin tab in the Launcher, select a category (e.g., 7KEYS) and choose a skin from the list.

Skin Configuration

Click "Settings" next to the skin dropdown to open specific options for that skin.

  • Lane Position: Adjust the horizontal position of lanes (Left, Center, Right).
  • Note Colors: Change colors for white/blue notes.
  • Video/BGA: Enable/Disable background videos.
  • Judge Position: Move the judgment line up or down.
  • Fast/Slow Display: Show early/late timing indicators.

Skin Installation

To install a new skin:

  1. Download the skin (usually a .zip or folder).
  2. Extract it into the skin/ folder in your beatoraja directory.
  3. Restart beatoraja or click "Reload Skin" in the Launcher.

Note: Some skins are designed for LR2 and may require specific settings to work perfectly in beatoraja.
