Hi-Speed Options

Scroll Speed Fix

  • START BPM: Lock scroll speed to the starting BPM of the song.
  • MAIN BPM: Lock scroll speed to the most common BPM (recommended).
  • MAX BPM: Lock scroll speed to the highest BPM in the song.
  • MIN BPM: Lock scroll speed to the lowest BPM.

Note: Adjust Hi-Speed in-game with START + TURNTABLE.

Green Number

The Green Number represents the visible duration of notes in milliseconds. Lower values mean faster scroll speed. Adjust with SELECT + TURNTABLE.

Lane Cover / Hidden / Lift

  • Lane Cover: Covers the top of the play area. Enable/Disable with Double Tap START.
  • Hidden: Notes disappear before reaching the judgment line.
  • Lift: Raises the judgment line from the bottom of the screen.

Gauge Types

  • ASSIST EASY: Easiest gauge. Recovery is high, damage is low. (60% to clear)
  • EASY: Easier than Normal. (80% to clear)
  • NORMAL (Groove): Standard gauge. Requires 80% at the end to clear.
  • HARD: Survival gauge. Starts at 100%. Game over at 0%. Does not recover on clear.
  • EX-HARD: Stricter survival gauge. Takes double damage.
  • HAZARD: Instant fail on combo break (Bad/Poor).

Gauge Auto Shift (GAS)

Automatically switches to an easier gauge if you fail a harder one mid-song.

  • SURVIVAL TO GROOVE: Switch from HARD to NORMAL upon hitting 0%.
  • BEST CLEAR: Start on EX-HARD and downgrade as needed to achieve the best possible clear.

Random Options

  • OFF: Original chart.
  • MIRROR: Flips the chart horizontally.
  • RANDOM: Randomly swaps lanes (except scratch).
  • R-RANDOM: Randomly rotates lanes. Maintains chords.
  • S-RANDOM: Randomly swaps every individual note. Breaks patterns completely.
  • SPIRAL: Shifts notes in a spiral pattern.

Assist Options

These options disable score saving but help with practice.

  • AUTO SCRATCH: Automatically plays scratch notes.
  • EXPAND JUDGE: Increases timing windows (Easy/Normal/Hard/Very Hard).
  • LEGACY NOTE: Converts Long Notes (LN) to standard notes.
  • CONSTANT: Ignores BPM changes and scrolls at a constant speed.
