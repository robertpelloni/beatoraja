Playing .osu Files

beatoraja includes built-in support for loading and playing Osu! beatmaps (.osu files), specifically those designed for Osu!mania.

How to Load

  1. Add your Osu! Songs folder to the Resource Tab in the Launcher.
  2. Alternatively, copy your Osu! beatmap folders into your beatoraja BMS directory.
  3. Click "Update BMS Database" to scan for .osu files.

Note: Only Osu!mania (Mode 3) and Standard (Mode 0, auto-converted) are fully supported.

Features

  • Key Counts: Supports 4K to 9K key modes automatically based on Circle Size (CS).
  • Long Notes (Sliders): Sliders are converted to Long Notes (LN).
  • Spinners: Spinners are mapped to the Scratch lane as Long Notes.
  • Hit Sounds: Supports custom hit sounds from the beatmap folder.
  • Background/Video: Displays background images and video events (if enabled in settings).

Configuration

In the Osu! tab of the Launcher, you can adjust specific settings:

Audio Settings

  • Hit Sound Volume: Adjust the volume of note hit sounds relative to the music.

Visual Settings

  • Background Dim: Darken the background image/video for better note visibility (0% to 100%).

Key Mapping

Key bindings for Osu! mode follow the standard BMS key configuration.

  • 4K: Uses the center 4 keys of the 7K layout (Keys 2, 3, 4, 5).
  • 7K: Uses the full 7K layout (Keys 1-7).
  • 8K: Uses 7K + Scratch.

You can customize these in the Input tab under "7KEYS".
