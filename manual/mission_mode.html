Mission Mode

← Back to Index

Overview

Mission Mode offers a series of challenges to test your skills and provide new goals. Complete missions to track your progress and achieve mastery.

Accessing Missions

  • In the Music Select screen, press F5 to open the Mod Menu.
  • Select the Mission tab.

Mission Types

There are two main categories of missions:

Normal Missions

Persistent challenges that can be completed at any time. These range from simple tasks (e.g., "Clear 10 Songs") to difficult achievements (e.g., "Full Combo a Level 12 Song").

Daily Missions

Time-limited missions that refresh every day. Complete these for regular practice goals.

  • Example: "Clear 3 songs today", "Achieve 500 Combo in one play".
  • Reset: Daily missions reset at midnight local time.

Progress & Completion

Tracking Progress

Your progress is updated automatically after every song. You can check your current status in the Mission tab.

Rewards

Completing missions grants satisfaction and bragging rights! Some future updates may include unlockable content or customization options.

Auto-Accept

By default, all missions are automatically accepted. You simply play the game, and if you meet the criteria, you make progress.
